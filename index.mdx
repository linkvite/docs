Linkvite is a bookmark manager. Save any link, note or file, organize it into collections, highlight what you read, and share collections with other people. It runs on the web, iOS and Android, with a browser extension for saving as you browse.

## Build on Linkvite

The Linkvite API covers everything the apps do: bookmarks, collections, highlights, reminders, RSS feeds, sharing and more. Official SDKs for JavaScript and Go are generated from the same specification as the [API reference](/api-reference).
