A collection is a folder of bookmarks. Each one is private to you until you share it.

## Create and organize collections

- Create a collection from the **Add** dialog (<kbd>C</kbd>) or the sidebar.
- Bookmarks without a collection are in **Unsorted** (<kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>U</kbd>).
- Pro accounts can nest collections inside other collections. The free plan includes 5 collections; see [plans](/guides/plans).

## Collaborate on a collection

Shared collections let several people save to the same place.

- **Invite people** by username or email, or create an **invite link** anyone can use to join. Invite links can expire and can be limited to a number of uses.
- **Roles**: every member is an **admin** or a **viewer**. Admins can edit or delete the collection, invite people, create invite links and change members' roles.
- **Comments**: members can discuss any bookmark in a shared collection.
- **See who's online**: the collection shows which members are viewing it right now.

Invitations you've received are under **Invites** (<kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>I</kbd>).

## Make a collection public

A public collection appears on your profile at `linkvite.io/<username>`, where anyone can browse it without an account. Every public collection also has an RSS feed, so people can follow it in a feed reader.

To hide your profile and its collections from everyone else, turn on **Private account** in the privacy section of **Settings**.
