## Billing

- **Trial**: 14 days free on the monthly plan, 30 days on the yearly plan. You aren't charged until the trial ends.
- **Changing plans**: upgrades are charged immediately. If you downgrade to Free, you keep Pro until the end of the billing period.
- **Refunds**: cancel within 7 days for a full refund.
- **Payment**: all major credit cards, processed by Stripe.

## Downgrading

Nothing is deleted. Everything you saved stays; you can't go past the free limits (like a sixth collection) until you upgrade again.
