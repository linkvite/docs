<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Linkvite for free?">
    Yes. The free plan has unlimited bookmarks, 5 collections, shared collections with comments, 3 reminders and 200 MB of file storage, with no time limit. See [plans](/guides/plans).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which plan is right for me?">
    Free covers personal use and sharing with others. Pro is for people who save a lot: unlimited collections and sub-collections, full-text search, permanent copies, and API and AI-assistant access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do you offer a free trial?">
    Yes: 14 days on the monthly plan and 30 days on the yearly plan. You aren't charged until the end of your trial.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel or change my plan at any time?">
    Yes. Upgrades are charged immediately. If you downgrade to Free, you keep your current plan until the end of the billing period. Cancel within 7 days for a full refund.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my things if I downgrade?">
    Nothing is deleted. Everything you saved stays; you just can't go past the free limits (like a sixth collection) until you upgrade again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What payment methods do you accept?">
    All major credit cards, processed securely through Stripe.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I bring my bookmarks from another app?">
    Yes. Linkvite imports HTML and CSV exports from browsers and apps like Raindrop.io and Pocket, and plain lists of links. See [import bookmarks](/guides/import).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I save private links or IP addresses?">
    Yes. Linkvite saves any link, including private pages and raw IP addresses, not only public websites.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
