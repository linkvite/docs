## Report a bug

Email [contact@linkvite.io](mailto:contact@linkvite.io) with:

- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
- the app you were using (web, iOS, Android or the extension) and its version;
- a screenshot or recording, if you can;
- the **request ID**, if an error showed one. Every API response carries one in the `X-Request-ID` header, and error responses include it as `request_id`. It lets us find exactly what went wrong.
